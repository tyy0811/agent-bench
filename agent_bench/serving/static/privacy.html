Privacy note

The demo box on this page runs entirely in your browser against canned responses: questions you type are never sent to a server, and the request log shown is a static sample plus your own browser-local entries, cleared on reload. Requests made directly to the live API endpoints (/ask, /ask/stream) are processed to generate an answer and recorded in a server-side audit log (question text, hashed client IP, safety verdicts, token and latency counts) kept for abuse monitoring and debugging, alongside aggregate service metrics. Nothing is used for analytics, profiling, or model training, and nothing is shared with third parties.
